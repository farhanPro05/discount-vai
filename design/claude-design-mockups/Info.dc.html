About Discount Vai

We started Discount Vai with one simple idea: good clothes should not cost a fortune, and buying them should feel like getting advice from your brother.

Team or workshop photo

What we make

Everyday menswear and unisex bags: trousers, shorts, 3/4 and half pants, drop-shoulder tees, club jerseys, jackets and Panjabi. A women's line is on the way.

Fair prices

We keep margins honest and pass the savings on. No fake "was" prices.

Quality checked

Every piece is checked for stitching, colour and size before it ships.

Here to help

Real people on WhatsApp and phone, every day from 9 am to 10 pm.

How we work

We sell online only, so we can skip rent and pass that on to you. Order with Cash on Delivery anywhere in Bangladesh, or pay with bKash, Nagad or card, and exchange within 7 days if it isn't right.

Returns & Exchange

Wrong size or not quite right? Exchange it within 7 days of delivery. No drama.

How to exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or the Contact page with your order number.
  2. Pick a new size or item. We hold it for you.
  3. Hand over the item to our rider, unworn with tags on.
  4. Get the new one in the same trip or within 2 days.

What can be exchanged

Unworn items with original tags and packaging. Sale items can be exchanged for a different size.

What can't

Items that have been worn, washed or altered, and innerwear for hygiene reasons.

Refunds

If we can't swap it, we refund to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to bKash or Nagad.
