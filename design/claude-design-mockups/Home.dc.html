About Discount Vai

Discount Vai is a Bangladeshi online clothing shop for men, with unisex bags and a women's line on the way. We make everyday trousers, shorts, drop-shoulder tees, club jerseys, jackets and Panjabi in breathable fabrics, and sell them at prices that make sense.

Order online with Cash on Delivery anywhere in Bangladesh, or pay with bKash, Nagad or card. Delivery inside Dhaka takes 1 to 2 days and outside Dhaka 2 to 4 days. If the size is not right, exchange it within 7 days. Think of us as your brother in the business: honest about quality, fair on price.
